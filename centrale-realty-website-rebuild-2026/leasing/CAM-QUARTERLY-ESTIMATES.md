# Quarterly maintenance (CAM) estimates, 2490 Walton Blvd

Source: L&R Homes quarterly statements for 2025 (billed Apr 8, Jul 21, Oct 9 2025, Jan 14 2026).
Internal reference. PRIVATE: give prospects only the Suite 101 / Suite 100 figures below, never other tenants' bills, balances or names.

## How it is billed
Each tenant pays a pro-rata share (by sq ft) of the building's common-area costs. Suite 101 (Lucrative Home Loans, 956 sq ft) is billed the same way every quarter. Line items: Terminex (pest), janitorial, tissue/cleaning supplies, trash, water/sewer, Detroit Edison (common areas), snow removal/salt, lawn service, sprinkler, and occasional one-offs.
