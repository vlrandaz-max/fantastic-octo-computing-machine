CENTRALE REALTY - MLS BRAND KIT

1) Start with a logo (recommended 525 x 150 px, 10 MB max)
   centrale-logo-on-dark-525x150.png    white wordmark + gold mark, transparent background.
                                        Use this if the MLS shows the logo on the dark (black) Primary color.
   centrale-logo-on-light-525x150.png   black wordmark + deep-gold accents, transparent background.
                                        Use this if the MLS shows the logo on white or a light background.
   The -1050x300.png files are the same logos at 2x for sharper display. Upload the 525x150 first;
   if the MLS accepts larger files, the 1050x300 will look crisper.

2) Choose your colors
   Primary   #0B0B0B   (banners and headers)
   Secondary #BAA383   (buttons)
   Accent    #7D6849   (smaller details)
   Cream #F6F3EE is the site's background color if the MLS asks for a light neutral.
   centrale-colors.png shows all three with their values.

3) Review the Brand Kit, then save.
   If the logo looks lost on the preview, switch to the other logo version.
